Scales, help on SELECT, nine new effect commands, a metronome, and SoundFonts that now cost only the sound you play.

• A SCALE screen — 38 built-in scales, a project key, and every note pulled onto the scale as it plays
• LPF/HPF/BPF, DRV, CRU, FIN and TSX — filters, drive, crush, fine tuning and transpose, written per note
• A metronome on SETTINGS, and TAP on PROJECT to set the tempo by ear
• Only the chosen sound loads out of a SoundFont — huge banks are usable now
• SELECT explains whatever cell the cursor is on
• A slow load says what it is doing, and B stops it
