No storage permission any more. On first run you grant one folder and that folder is its home; you can add more later.

• Mixer automation: VTR/VMV fader effects, and AUS/AUF to ramp any of them over time
• R+UP / R+DOWN zooms the sample editor
• R+LEFT / R+RIGHT moves the text cursor fast
• L+R clears the copy buffer
• Button click records at the volume you hear
• .sf3 SoundFonts are no longer offered; .sf2 is unaffected
• Also released for Windows, Linux and Linux handhelds
