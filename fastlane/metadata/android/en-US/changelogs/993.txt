What's new:

• MACRO: a synth instrument with 47 models, based on Braids
• Envelopes in milliseconds, with curves; more ways to modulate
• Three new filters: LD, K35 and a notch
• Three fonts, and capitals, small letters or Title case
• Hints in the top panel: what the cursor is on and what the buttons do
• Pick an instrument, a model or a SoundFont sound from a list
• Start playing from the cursor on CHAIN and SONG
